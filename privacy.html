Privacy

Clearline is free SAT practice. You never need an account, and we keep as little about you as we can.

Under 13? Please don't sign in. Clearline works fully without an account; your progress stays in your browser.

If you don't sign in

Your answers, scores, notes and settings are saved only in this browser on this device. They are never sent to us.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. It lets your progress follow you to another device. We store:

  • a random id we make for you,
  • your Google account's id and email address, only so we can recognize you when you sign in,
  • your Clearline progress (your answers, practice-test results, notes and settings),
  • when your account was made and when your progress was last saved.

We don't ask Google for your name or photo, and we never collect your birthday, school, location or anything else.

When you sign out, Clearline clears the copy in that browser, so the next person on a shared computer starts fresh. Your progress is still in your account and comes back when you sign in.

Who can see it

Only you, when you're signed in. Your progress is never sold, shared or used for ads. It is kept in a database run by Neon, and the site runs on Vercel; they store it for us and can't use it for anything else.

Deleting it

  • Your account: open Settings, then Delete my account and data. Your account and the progress saved to it are deleted right away, for good.
  • This browser's copy: clear this site's data in your browser settings. Download a backup first (Settings, Back up my progress) if you want to keep it.

Visit counts

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't track you across sites.
